# Release Validation and Ship/Iterate Decision

Status: **pending.** Run this after the growth-release stories (#16–#21) are implemented and the baseline is complete.

## Method

- Repeat Tasks 1–3 of the [session script](session-script.md) with a **new** cohort of first-time players. Reusing baseline participants would measure learning, not the product.
- Use the same devices and viewport mix as the baseline where possible.
- Aim for at least the baseline sample size. Record the actual number.
- Use the same [observation log](observation-log-template.md) and [event dictionary](event-dictionary.md) so the rounds are comparable.

## Limitations

- Both cohorts are small, recruited, and moderated. Differences between them are directional, not statistically significant.
- Moderated sessions inflate completion compared with real, unobserved play.
- Share outcomes are what the share sheet reports. Nothing here shows a recipient played.
- Seven-day return is self-reported by the people who answered the follow-up.
