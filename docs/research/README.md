## Ground rules

- Observation and participant follow-up are the initial measurement method. No analytics service, identifiers, or tracking code is added.
- Keep reports anonymous. Use participant codes (P01, P02, …) and never commit names, contact details, recordings, or anything else that identifies a person.
- A small cohort shows where people struggle. It is not statistical proof of retention or virality.
